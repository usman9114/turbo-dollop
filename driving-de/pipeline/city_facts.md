# Vetted StVO facts for city driving (source of truth for the generator)

## Speeds
- Inside built-up areas the limit is 50 km/h. It starts at the yellow town sign (Ortstafel, Z 310), NOT at a speed sign, and ends at Z 311.
- Tempo-30-Zone (Z 274.1): max 30 km/h in the whole zone until Z 274.2. Inside such zones there are normally no priority signs, so "rechts vor links" applies at junctions, and usually no zebra crossings / traffic lights.
- Verkehrsberuhigter Bereich (Z 325.1, "Spielstraße"): Schrittgeschwindigkeit (walking pace, courts accept about 4–7 km/h, never more than ~10). Pedestrians may use the whole road, children may play everywhere. Parking only in marked spaces. When LEAVING it (Z 325.2 / onto a normal road) you must give way to everyone (§10 StVO).
- Being far too slow without reason is also wrong (§3(2)) and examiners mark it.

## Right of way (Vorfahrt)
- No signs, no lights → rechts vor links (§8): give way to vehicles coming from your right — including cyclists.
- §10 StVO: whoever enters the road from a driveway, parking lot, field track, over a lowered kerb (abgesenkter Bordstein) or out of a verkehrsberuhigter Bereich must give way to ALL other road users. Rechts vor links does not apply in their favour.
- Order of precedence: police officer's signals > traffic lights > signs > general rules (e.g. rechts vor links).
- Z 205 (inverted triangle): give way. Z 206 (STOP): full stop at the stop line (Haltlinie) or, without line, where you can see the crossing traffic — a rolling stop is a violation and an instant test fail. Then give way.
- Z 306 Vorfahrtstraße (yellow diamond): you have priority at all following junctions until Z 307. If the priority road bends (Zusatzzeichen showing the "abknickende Vorfahrt"), you must use your indicator when you follow the bend, and when you leave it going straight.
- Z 301: priority at the NEXT junction only.
- Z 102: warning – junction where rechts vor links applies.
- Roundabout (Z 215, usually with Z 205): traffic in the circle has priority; do NOT indicate when entering; you MUST indicate right before exiting. No stopping inside the roundabout.
- Z 208 (red arrow smaller): give priority to oncoming traffic at the narrow section. Z 308 (blue square, white arrow bigger): you have priority over oncoming traffic.

## Turning (§9)
- Routine before turning or pulling out: inner mirror → outer mirror → indicate → Schulterblick (shoulder check) → position (einordnen) → slow down → final Schulterblick → turn.
- Right turn: keep as far right as possible; cyclists and e-scooters riding straight on the cycle lane/path to your right have priority — the right-turn shoulder check is the most important check in German cities.
- Left turn: give way to oncoming traffic going straight or turning right (incl. trams). Get into position near the centre line (on one-way streets: the left edge).
- Two cars facing each other both turning left normally turn in front of each other ("voreinander", tangential), unless traffic/junction layout requires passing first.
- When turning, pedestrians crossing the road you are turning into have priority (also at green lights).

## Traffic lights
- Amber (Gelb): stop if you can do so safely before the stop line.
- Grünpfeil sign (Z 720, metal green arrow on black next to the red light): turning right on red is allowed ONLY after a full stop at the stop line, and you must not hinder or endanger pedestrians, cyclists or crossing traffic. A green ARROW LIGHT (lit green arrow) is different: it is a normal green light for that direction (protected turn).
- No parking/halting within 10 m before a traffic light if you would hide it.

## Pedestrians, cyclists, buses, trams
- Zebra crossing (Z 350 sign, Z 293 marking): you must let pedestrians (and wheelchair users) cross who are clearly waiting or crossing; approach at moderate speed, no overtaking before the zebra, no halting on it or within 5 m before it.
- Overtaking cyclists/e-scooters/pedestrians: minimum 1.5 m lateral distance inside built-up areas, 2 m outside (since 2020).
- Schutzstreifen (cycle lane with DASHED line): cars may cross it only when needed; halting on it is forbidden. Radfahrstreifen (SOLID line, with Z 237): cars must not drive or halt on it.
- Many one-way streets allow cyclists in both directions (Z 220 / Z 267 with Zusatzzeichen "Radverkehr frei") — expect oncoming bikes.
- Fahrradstraße (Z 244.1): for bicycles; other vehicles only if a Zusatzzeichen allows them; then max 30 km/h, cyclists may ride side by side and must not be hindered.
- Bus approaching a stop with hazard lights on: must not be overtaken. Bus/tram standing at a stop with hazard lights: passing (in both directions) only at walking pace and at a distance that does not endanger passengers; stop if necessary. Buses pulling out of a marked stop must be allowed to leave (wait if needed).
- Tram stopped at a stop without a traffic island: pass on the right only at walking pace and only if passengers are not endangered; otherwise wait.
- Trams are overtaken on the right; on one-way streets also on the left.
- Children, elderly, people needing help (§3(2a)): reduce speed and be ready to brake.

## Halting and parking (§12)
- Halten = stopping up to 3 minutes or to load/unload/let people in or out. Leaving the car or more than 3 min = Parken.
- No parking within 5 m before and after junctions (measured from the intersection of the kerb lines), in front of driveways (and opposite them on narrow roads), in front of lowered kerbs.
- No parking 15 m before and after a bus stop sign (Z 224).
- Z 283 absolute Haltverbot: no stopping at all. Z 286 eingeschränktes Haltverbot: halting up to 3 min for loading/passengers allowed, no parking.
- Park on the right side, in the direction of travel (on one-way streets both sides).

## Other key rules
- Zipper merge (Reißverschluss, §7(4)): when a lane ends, use it until the end and merge alternately right at the merge point.
- Blue lights + siren: immediately clear the way ("sofort freie Bahn schaffen").
- Rettungsgasse: on motorways and on roads outside towns with at least two lanes per direction, as soon as traffic is at walking pace or stopped: leftmost lane goes left, all others go right.
- Phone: holding/using a phone while driving is forbidden, also when stopped at a red light; only allowed with the engine fully switched off (not auto start-stop).
- Winter tyres: required in wintry conditions (black ice, snow, slush, frost). Only tyres with the Alpine symbol (3PMSF, mountain with snowflake) count since 1 Oct 2024; "M+S" alone no longer counts.
- Level crossing with St Andrew's cross (Z 201): trains always have priority. Do not overtake from the first countdown marker (Bake) before a level crossing.

## Lesson-day practicalities
- Bring your driving licence (and glasses if your licence requires them).
- Tell the instructor up front what you want: multi-lane junctions, trams, cyclists on the right, rechts vor links in 30-zones, parallel/reverse parking, merging.
- Do the cockpit drill each time: seat, steering wheel, head rest, mirrors, seat belt.
- "Kommentiertes Fahren": say out loud what you see and plan — it makes your scanning visible to the instructor.
- Typical mistakes of experienced drivers new to German city traffic: missing rechts vor links in 30-zones, forgetting the right-turn shoulder check for cyclists, rolling through STOP signs, stopping on zebra crossings or bike lanes, accelerating through amber, not slowing for buses with hazard lights, speeding after a 30-zone ends without noticing that 50 applies again.
